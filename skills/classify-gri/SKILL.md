---
name: classify-gri
description: Apply GRI 1-6 in order to pick heading, subheading and tariff line.
triggers: [classify, heading, subheading, which code]
---
1. GRI 1: classify by the terms of the headings and any section/chapter notes. Most goods end here.
2. GRI 2: incomplete/unassembled articles and mixtures follow the complete article or material rules.
3. GRI 3: if two headings fit, prefer the most specific; then essential character; then the last in numerical order.
4. GRI 4: only for goods not classifiable above (most akin).
5. GRI 5: cases and packing for the article. GRI 6: compare subheadings only at the same level.
Always compare against at least one alternative code and state why it was rejected.
